# Helm v1 BOM

| Item | Qty | Notes |
|------|-----|-------|
| Seeed Studio XIAO RP2040 | 2 | One for the build, one spare for brick insurance |
| Gateron MX switches | 6 | Any MX-compatible; buy a 10-pack for extras |
| EC11 rotary encoder with push switch | 1 | D-shaped shaft + aluminum knob |
| 1u PBT blank keycaps | 6 | MX stem |
| M2 brass heat-set inserts | 4 | Length ≈ 3–4 mm, OD ≈ 3.5 mm |
| M2 pan-head screws | 4 | 6–8 mm length to match insert depth + plate |
| 26–28 AWG stranded wire | ~2 m | For direct-pin flying leads |
| Dark PETG filament | ~50 g | For `helm_plate.stl` and `helm_bottom.stl` |
| Rubber feet | 4 | Optional, on the bottom corners |

## Optional but recommended

- Flux and no-clean solder for the XIAO castellated pads
- Flush cutters for trimming the EC11 legs
- A USB-C data cable (some charge-only cables will not enumerate)
