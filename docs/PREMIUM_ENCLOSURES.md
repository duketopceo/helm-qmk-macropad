# Premium Enclosure Comparison (quote brief)

Date: 2026-11-08. No quotes collected yet. This document is the brief to send to suppliers, not a comparison of prices.

## Options to quote (comparable)

1. Home FDM shell (existing PETG two-piece)
2. Outsourced polymer printing (same STL geometry)
3. Metal top plate + printed lower shell (hybrid)
4. Fully machined housing
5. Injection-molded shell

Request quantities: 5, 25, 100 per option.

## For each quote, record separately

Engineering, tooling, setup, per-part cost, finish, freight, tax, lead time, revision exposure (cost of a geometry change), minimum order.

## Decision rule

Metal or molded does not automatically mean better. A premium enclosure must beat the FDM shell on retention, feel, and repeatability at the quoted quantity. Test the hybrid metal-top concept mechanically (plate stiffness, encoder mount, insert bosses) before committing to machining or tooling quotes that assume an unproven geometry.
